 Privacy Policy
Mya Hijabs operates this website on the Shopify platform and is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website myahijabs.com . By using our Site, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
1. Information We Collect
We collect personal information when you visit our Site, place an order, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us. The types of personal information we may collect include:
Name
Email address
Shipping and billing address
Phone number
Payment information (processed securely via Shopify Payments or third-party payment providers)
Order history
Any other information you voluntarily provide
We may also collect non-personal information such as your browser type, IP address, and browsing behavior through cookies and similar technologies used by Shopify.
2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
Process and fulfill your orders through Shopify’s eCommerce platform
Communicate with you regarding your orders, promotions, and updates
Improve our website, products, and services
Prevent fraudulent transactions and enhance security
Comply with legal obligations
3. How We Share Your Information
We do not sell or rent your personal information. However, we may share your information with:
Shopify (our eCommerce platform) to process orders and payments
Service providers (e.g., payment processors, shipping carriers) to fulfill your orders
Legal authorities if required by law
Business transfers in case of a merger or acquisition
4. Data Security
Our Site is hosted on Shopify, which implements security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
5. Your Rights & Choices
You may:
Access, update, or delete your personal information by contacting us
Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time
Disable cookies through your browser settings
Manage your privacy settings through your Shopify account (if applicable)
6. Third-Party Links & Shopify Apps
Our Site may contain links to third-party websites or use Shopify apps that collect additional data. We are not responsible for their privacy policies or content.
7. Children's Privacy
Our Site is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
