 Embrace effortless elegance with our Rose Marble Printed Hijab. Inspired by the natural luxury of fine stone, this exquisite scarf features a seamless transition from a soft dusty rose blush near the face into a striking, multi-toned marble pattern draped below. Detailed with delicate veins of terracotta, copper, and soft grey, it adds a sophisticated, artistic touch to any outfit.
Crafted from premium, lightweight fabric, this printed long scarf offers a beautiful, fluid drape that stays secure all day. Whether you are dressing up for a formal event or adding a polished finish to your everyday modest wear, the Rose Marble hijab provides the perfect balance of breathability, opacity, and timeless style.
Product Highlights & Features:
Unique Artistic Design: A beautiful blend of soft rose quartz pink and earth-toned marble textures.
Premium Quality Fabric: High-quality, breathable, and lightweight material designed for an elegant drape.
Versatile Styling: Generous length allows for multiple styling options, from effortless casual drapes to high-coverage formal folds.
All-Season Wear: Keeps you cool in the summer and layers beautifully for cooler seasons.
Specifications & Care:
Material: Premium Modal
Shape: Long Rectangle
Dimensions: Approx. 70" x 28" (180cm x 70cm)
Care Instructions: Hand wash in cold water with mild detergent. Hang to dry. Low iron or steam if needed
