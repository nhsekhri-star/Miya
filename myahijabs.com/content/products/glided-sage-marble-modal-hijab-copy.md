 Make a bold statement in effortless luxury. This striking hijab features a dramatic, multi-tonal abstract marble print, layering deep charcoal tones with soft, ethereal waves of sage and brilliant flecks of golden-olive. It’s an artistic, high-contrast piece designed to add instant depth and luxury to your look.
Woven from our signature premium modal fabric, it delivers a buttery-soft feel against your skin, an elegant, fluid drape, and breathable comfort that carries you beautifully from day to night.
Why You’ll Love It:
 Premium Modal Quality: Incredibly soft, breathable, and intentionally lightweight for a premium touch.
 Artistic Marble Print: A sophisticated blend of dark onyx tones, soft sage waves, and rich olive-gold accents.
 Flawless Drape: Holds its style beautifully with a natural, effortless flow and minimal slipping.
 Versatile Statement Piece: Easily dressed up for formal occasions or styled casually to elevate your everyday neutrals.
Product Details:
 Brand: mya hijabs
 Material: 100% Premium Modal
 Texture: Smooth, ultra-soft, and lightweight
 Size: Standard rectangle for versatile coverage and styling
 Care Instructions: Hand wash cold or machine wash on a delicate cycle in a laundry bag. Hang to dry. Iron on low heat if needed.
